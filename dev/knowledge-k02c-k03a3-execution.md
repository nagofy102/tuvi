## Acceptance

- K02C separates official metadata, full-text clauses, calendar conventions and product
  profile policy; it calls a field verified only when an admitted source supports it.
- K03A3 records a substantive rule only with a lawful, page/section-level locator,
  bounded text policy, condition, input facts, conclusion, exceptions, profile and
  proposed negative tests. AI/BA synthesis is separately labelled.
- Knowledge Yield reports substantive usable rules rather than document count.
- Methodology/orchestration records have no claim kind and never authorize a claim.
- K04B remains postponed until PO receives sufficient substantive, traceable material.
