## Admission conditions for a deeper reading

The leap passage includes `凡有闰月具要依此为例` after the leap-first-month
example: the transcription explicitly generalizes the example to leap months.
It therefore cannot be treated as agreement with either app leap option merely
because the worked example mentions month 1. No leap case is included in this
comparison corpus until the incompatible profiles are separately specified.

1. A rule has a stable ID, exact excerpt IDs, own Vietnamese paraphrase, scope,
   explicit geometric predicate and excluded claims. Source existence alone does
   not prove a rule is empirically true.
2. The actual chart supplies at least two distinct relevant stars or independently
   identified Mệnh/Thân contexts. Never conclude from one isolated star.
3. Tam phương tứ chính membership is an observed chart relation, not a blanket
   permission to copy every classical same-palace statement. Pair-context
   synthesis must say when it is editorially broader than the literal excerpt.
4. Each displayed synthesis exposes “Vì sao luận như vậy”: actual stars, actual
   palace/branch/relation, source excerpt and the limited inference made.
5. Missing, disputed or unverified prerequisites suppress or qualify the rule.
   No unsupported luck percentages, predictions, medical diagnoses or paid rituals.

## Independent comparison corpus

`../tests/fixtures/reference-charts.json` contains twenty-four **partial,
manual-source-derived** lunar-input fixtures, not twenty-four independently certified
complete charts. Mệnh covers all twelve branches by varying the twelve hours of
the first lunar month. Eleven further cases count months 2–12 at hour Tý;
the last case combines month 12 with hour Hợi. All twelve lunar months are covered.
Tả/Hữu and Xương/Khúc are hand-counted from the quoted
source, without calling any engine to obtain expected values. The first case also
records the source's literal Giáp-year, fire-cục, day-one Tử Vi example.

No person is represented: IDs identify synthetic cases and names are absent.
The fixture uses canonical `Tả Phụ`; iztro's current Vietnamese locale spells the
same 左輔 star `Tả Phù`. An explicit label alias is permissible; this is spelling
normalization, not a school-rule disagreement or a license to merge other stars.
No solar date is invented. Omitted fields (most Cục values, most principal stars,
all other auxiliary stars, dignity, Tứ Hóa, vận hạn) are explicitly unverified,
not null answers or automatic passes. A test runner may convert the lunar input
for engine invocation, but that conversion is not independently validated by this
corpus. Expected values must remain these externally sourced manual counts;
engine snapshots can be regression checks only, never oracle replacements.

Coverage gaps: independent published complete charts, leap months, late Tý,
the full month-by-hour cross product, alternate schools, full fourteen principal stars and all
auxiliary placements. This corpus is useful bounded evidence and does not close
the older 200–300 independently reviewed complete-chart acceptance gate.
