## Palace scope

Volume 2's `安十二宫例` explicitly gives the twelve-palace sequence:
命、兄弟、妻妾、子女、财帛、疾厄、迁移、奴仆、官禄、田宅、福德、父母.
The application modernizes 妻妾 as Phu Thê and 奴仆 as Nô Bộc/relationships with
collaborators. Palace labels identify topics, not outcomes. Use the existing palace
catalog for topic descriptions and identify modern wording as editorial.

For Tật Ách, discuss routines, rest and wellbeing only, not diseases, disability,
lifespan or treatment. For Phu Thê/Tử Tức, discuss communication and expectations,
not fidelity, divorce, fertility or number/sex of children. For Tài Bạch/Điền Trạch,
discuss resource management, not investment returns or guaranteed ownership.

## Context and conflicts that must remain visible

- Volume 1 `太微賦` says `不可一概論議` (cannot discuss all alike), and asks for
  placement, dignity and interaction context. `增補太微賦` says `凶不皆凶，吉無純吉`.
  This supports a contextual, non-binary reading style within the tradition; it
  does not scientifically validate an algorithm or cancel harmful source content.
- Volume 2 `安身命例` describes month counting from Dần and inverse/forward hour
  counting for Mệnh/Thân. It also says `又若闰月正月生者要在二月内起安身命`:
  this transcription treats leap first month as the following month. It does **not**
  verify the project's current day-15 splitting policy. Preserve the selected
  engine profile and disclose this disagreement; never silently change birth data.
- Volume 2 includes alternative tables/rules (e.g. 四化 and life-stage sequencing)
  that must not be mixed unannounced with iztro defaults. No complete comparison or
  expert-grade golden fixture corpus has been produced here.
- Historical text contains sexist, classist, stigmatizing and deterministic claims,
  including predictions of death/crime/illness. Do not reproduce or soften these
  into personal judgments. Exclude such rule branches entirely.

## Implementation contract

1. Derive visible chart facts only from a successfully returned current engine
   chart; rejected calendar cases receive no interpretation.
2. Link each star theme to the chapter and heading actually read. Context/geometry
   facts (co-location, tam hợp, opposite palace) are computed facts, not proof of
   an independently sourced combination interpretation.
3. Label the report “Luận giải tham khảo theo quy tắc”, with its profile/version,
   provenance, limits and “Vì sao?” facts. Do not label deterministic prose as a
   live AI response or invent external-provider integration.
4. Show combinations as multiple symbolic perspectives unless a combination rule
   has its own documented source; do not manufacture named classical configurations.
5. No fortune scores, annual predictions, diagnosis, fear-based recommendations or
   paid rituals. No claims of human expert sign-off or scientific prediction.
6. Interpretation generation must not change calendar, star placements or engine
   approval status. Technical tests can verify reproducibility/source completeness,
   not confirm a person's future or personality.
